# DEV/MLH Side Hustle Week 2

Official rules checked October 14, 2026:

- [Challenge](https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week2-2026-10-12)
- [Contest rules](https://dev.to/page/hacktoberfest-week2-2026-10-12-contest-rules)

The entry window runs October 12 at 12:00 PDT through October 18 at 23:59 PDT.
A new project and repository, open-source/open-weight AI central to the product,
an English DEV post, and a video or deployed demonstration are required.
AI assistance is allowed. Eligibility and final declarations must be checked by
the entrant. Personal identity and payment documents do not belong in this repo.

Prizes are competitive: one $250, six $200 and eleven $100 awards, at most one
per entrant. No sponsor category is claimed for Subcheck. Published reward terms
are not an award or payment confirmation. No DEV entry has been submitted.
